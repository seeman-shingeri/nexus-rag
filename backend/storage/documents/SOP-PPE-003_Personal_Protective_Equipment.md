# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-PPE-003
## Topic: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance in Active Machinery Zones

### 1. Mandatory Zone Standards
1. **Zone A (Machinery Bay)**: All individuals entering the boundary must wear ANSI Z89.1 certified Class E hardhats and high-visibility reflective vests.
2. **Eye and Hearing Protection**: Impact goggles and ear defense (NRR 25+) are compulsory within 5 meters of active CNC milling stations or hydraulic press HP-400.
3. **Footwear**: Steel-toe puncture-resistant boots are mandatory across all industrial floors.

### 2. Detection of Non-Compliance
1. When computer vision algorithms detect an individual without a hardhat or high-visibility vest in Zone A for more than 3 seconds, a warning event is registered.
2. The operator is prompted via local visual status beacon to retrieve equipment from the adjacent PPE locker.
3. Persistent non-compliance exceeding 2 minutes results in formal notification sent to the shift safety supervisor.

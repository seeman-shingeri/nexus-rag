# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-SEC-101
## Topic: Access Control and Physical Security for Chemical Storage Vault (Zone B)

### 1. Purpose and Scope
Establishes perimeter physical security, badge verification, and authorized personnel limits for Zone B (Chemical Storage Vault).

### 2. Authorization Requirements
1. **Clearance Badging**: Entry into Zone B requires Tier-3 Hazardous Materials Security Clearance. Unescorted visitors, subcontractors, and general assembly operators are strictly forbidden from entering.
2. **Two-Person Rule**: Due to high vapor pressure solvents, entry requires a minimum of two certified operators equipped with localized gas monitors. Single-person lone worker entry is strictly prohibited.
3. **Maximum Occupancy**: The vault is engineered for a maximum simultaneous occupancy of 2 individuals.

### 3. Intrusion and Unauthorized Access Response
1. When visual perception sensors detect unauthorized personnel crossing the vault threshold, NEXUS issues an immediate CRITICAL safety alert.
2. The facility dispatch console broadcasts an automated audio advisory informing the individual to withdraw to the staging vestibule immediately.
3. If the individual fails to withdraw within 30 seconds, security personnel are automatically dispatched and the secondary electromagnetic door latch engages.

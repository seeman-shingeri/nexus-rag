# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-FIRE-008
## Topic: Emergency Egress Corridor Maintenance and Obstruction Mitigation (Zone D)

### 1. Purpose and Policy
In accordance with international life safety codes, all emergency egress corridors, fire exit portals, and assembly walkways must remain clear of physical obstructions, pallets, mobile machinery, and storage boxes 24 hours a day.

### 2. Egress Clearance Standards
1. **Corridor Width**: The central egress pathway through Zone D must maintain an unhindered clearance width of not less than 2.4 meters.
2. **Zero Storage Rule**: Storing empty wooden pallets, waste disposal bins, or unanchored tool carts within 3 meters of Fire Exit Door ED-04 is a code violation.
3. **Overhead Clearance**: Emergency exit signs and illumination fixtures must have unobstructed sightlines from at least 30 meters distance.

### 3. Automated Detection and Enforcement
1. When visual monitoring identifies an obstruction persisting for more than 4 consecutive seconds, a high-priority alert is issued to the floor safety marshal.
2. The safety marshal must inspect and remove the obstruction within 60 seconds of notification.
3. If unaddressed after 3 minutes, the alert escalates to the Plant Operations Manager.

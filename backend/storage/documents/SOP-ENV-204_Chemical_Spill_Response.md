# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-ENV-204
## Topic: Rapid Response to Chemical and Liquid Spills in Industrial Workstations

### 1. Purpose and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines mandatory protocols for responding to liquid puddles, solvent leaks, lubricant discharge, and chemical spills within industrial work bays and machine rooms.

### 2. Immediate Initial Actions
1. **Visual Confirmation**: Immediately upon visual identification of pooling liquid near machinery or pedestrian walkways, operators must mark the boundary with hazard cones.
2. **Electrical Hazard Assessment**: Check proximity to electrical feeds, junction boxes, and grounded tooling. If liquid approaches energized conduits within 1.5 meters, immediately trigger emergency electrical shut-off (E-Stop).
3. **Deploy Containment Booms**: Retrieve chemical containment booms from the nearest yellow spill locker (Station NS-02). Surround the outer perimeter of the spill to prevent migration toward floor drains or egress pathways.
4. **Ventilation**: If the substance exhibits pungent vapor, solvent smell, or fuming, immediately engage auxiliary overhead extraction hoods and notify the shift supervisor.

### 3. Neutralization and Absorbent Application
- For hydrocarbon lubricants and oils: Apply non-combustible diatomaceous clay absorbent over the puddle center. Allow 5 minutes dwell time.
- For acidic or caustic solvents: Deploy neutralizing powder from the designated spill kit until the visual pH indicator registers neutral yellow/green.
- Prohibited Action: Never use plain water to flush unknown liquids into common floor drains.

### 4. Incident Escalation and Reporting
All spills exceeding 5 liters must be formally logged in the NEXUS Incident Center with photo evidence, volume estimate, and assigned cleanup marshal.
